# FixPilot AI Maintenance Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

FixPilot AI Maintenance is designed as an offline-friendly property maintenance app. The current app stores property, maintenance issue, inspection, repair cost, reminder, report, and photo evidence data locally on the user's device.

## Data Collection

The current version does not collect personal data on developer-operated servers and does not send personal data to third-party AI services.

The app may store user-entered content locally on device, including property details, maintenance notes, inspection records, repair cost records, and photos selected or captured by the user.

## Photos and Camera

Camera and photo library access are used only when the user chooses to attach photo evidence to a maintenance issue or inspection record.

## Notifications

Local notifications may be used for maintenance reminders. These reminders are scheduled on device.

## AI Features

AI features run in mock mode in this release. FixPilot does not send issue notes, property details, inspection notes, repair records, photos, or any other personal data to OpenAI, ChatGPT, or any third-party AI service.

If a future version enables remote AI processing, the app will first explain what data is sent, identify who receives it, request user permission before sending it, and update this privacy policy to describe collection, use, sharing, and protection of that data.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by Apple's StoreKit and App Store systems.
